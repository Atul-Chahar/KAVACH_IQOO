# expected: HIGH_RISK (>=70) | families: AUTHORITY_IMPERSONATION, URGENCY_AND_THREAT, ISOLATION_AND_SECRECY, REMOTE_ACCESS_AND_TRANSFER, CREDENTIAL_EXTRACTION
# ~75s spoken, English, two speakers. The live-demo script from docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md.
#
# Written to escalate one family per beat rather than to be realistic prose: the
# point is that a room full of people can watch the band change on cue and check
# the reason against what was just said. Amber lands on beat 2, red on beat 3.

CALLER: Good afternoon. This is Inspector Sharma from the CBI. Your Aadhaar is linked to a money laundering case.
VICTIM: Sorry, who is this?
CALLER: An arrest warrant has been issued against you. This is a non bailable offence. You will be arrested within two hours.
VICTIM: I have not done anything.
CALLER: You are under digital arrest. Do not tell anyone, not even your family. Keep your camera on and do not disconnect this call.
VICTIM: Okay. Okay, I am listening.
CALLER: For clearance you must transfer to this account. It is a safe custody account and it is fully refundable. Now install AnyDesk and turn screen sharing on.
VICTIM: How much are we talking about?
CALLER: Read me the code that just arrived on your phone. Tell me the OTP. And confirm your UPI PIN.
