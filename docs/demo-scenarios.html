Running one

  1. Phone on speakerphone, on the table. Kavach hears the room, not the call — on earpiece it hears only your half.
  2. The second person reads the Caller lines. Pause about two seconds between turns; the recogniser settles an utterance at a silence.
  3. Read at a normal pace. The underlined phrases are the ones that score — say those exactly.
  4. Follow a scam with a genuine call. A detector that only ever says "scam" is a smoke alarm taped to the on position.
underlined — a phrase that scores dotted — a guard phrase that subtracts Red needs three different families, not one loud one.

Scam scripts

Ordered by how fast they reach red. Each opens differently on purpose — if a judge asks for another scenario, the next one down is not the same trick again.

The five-beat opener

Purpose-built to escalate one family per beat. Fastest to red. Open with this.

Warns · 100 amber 12s · red 24s
AuthorityUrgencyIsolationMoney / remote accessCredentials
Caller

Good afternoon. This is Inspector Sharma from the CBI. Your Aadhaar is linked to a money laundering case.

You

Sorry, who is this?

Caller

An arrest warrant has been issued against you. This is a non bailable offence. You will be arrested within two hours.

You

I have not done anything.

Caller

You are under digital arrest. Do not tell anyone, not even your family. Keep your camera on and do not disconnect this call.

You

Okay. Okay, I am listening.

Caller

For clearance you must transfer to this account. It is a safe custody account and it is fully refundable. Now install AnyDesk and turn screen sharing on.

You

How much are we talking about?

Caller

Read me the code that just arrived on your phone. Tell me the OTP. And confirm your UPI PIN.

“Your son has been detained”

Aimed at parents. Emotional pressure, secrecy, money — and it never asks for a credential.

Warns · 100 amber 12s · red 24s
AuthorityUrgencyIsolationMoney / remote access
Caller

Madam, I am Sub Inspector Verma. Your son has been detained in a narcotics case and an FIR has been registered.

You

What? Is he alright?

Caller

He is here at the station. This is a non bailable matter and he will be arrested formally within two hours.

You

Please, I want to speak to him.

Caller

Not yet. Do not tell anyone, especially not other family members, and do not disconnect this call.

You

I understand.

Caller

To settle this quietly you can transfer to this account. Treat it as an advance payment against the bail, fully refundable.

The customs parcel

The FedEx variant — the most reported opening after digital arrest.

Warns · 100 amber 24s · red 24s
AuthorityUrgencyIsolationMoney / remote access
Caller

Good morning, I am calling from the customs department at Mumbai airport. An illegal parcel has been intercepted in your name.

You

A parcel? I have not sent anything.

Caller

The parcel contains narcotics. Your Aadhaar is linked to the shipping documents, so a case of money laundering has been registered.

You

That cannot be right.

Caller

Sir, legal action will be taken within two hours unless this is resolved. Do not tell anyone about this, and do not disconnect the call.

You

What do I have to do?

Caller

You will need to transfer to this account for verification. It is a refundable amount, held only until your name is cleared.

What activity?

You

Okay.

Calls that must stay calm

These are the ones worth demonstrating unprompted. Each says something a naive keyword detector would alarm on, and each stays quiet for a reason you can point at.

A real officer about your own FIR

Says “Sub Inspector”, “FIR” and “police station” out loud. Authority alone is not evidence.

Stays calm · 28 never reaches amber
Caller

Good afternoon, this is Sub Inspector Kulkarni from the police station. You filed a complaint about a stolen phone last week.

You

Yes, I did.

Caller

Your FIR has been registered and the handset has been traced. I need you to come and identify it when you are free.

You

How do I know this is genuine?

Caller

Fair question. This call is recorded for quality. You can call us back on the station landline listed on your copy of the FIR, and your reference number for this call is 4471.

You

Thank you, I will come tomorrow.

Caller

Take your time. Bring your ID, and you can discuss with your family before coming if you prefer. We never ask for money or an OTP on the phone.

A courier asking for the delivery OTP

A legitimate call that asks, correctly, for an OTP. One family — however loud — cannot alarm.

Caller

No hurry, take your time.

You

Yes, I have it here.

Caller

Please read me the code so I can mark it delivered. If you prefer, you can hand it to me at the door instead.

You

Here you go.

Caller

Thank you, the package is yours. Have a good day.

A real bank asking for a KYC update

Trips the same fraud-desk marker the scam does, then defuses itself.

Stays calm · 12 never reaches amber

You

Do I need to do something online?

Caller

No. Please visit your nearest branch with your ID at any time in the next month. There is no deadline pressure and nothing is blocked.

You

Can you not do it over the phone?

Caller

We will never ask for your OTP, PIN or password over a call, so no. If you would rather confirm this is us, you can call us back on the number on your card.

You

That is reassuring.

Caller

Your reference number for this call is 90218. Take your time, and you can discuss with your family before visiting.

A real fraud desk about a real transaction

The hardest negative: it genuinely trips authority and urgency, then hands back control.

Stays calm · 12 never reaches amber
Caller

Good afternoon, I am calling from your bank's fraud monitoring team regarding a transaction on your card ending 4471.

You

Okay, go ahead.

Caller

We flagged a transaction of eighteen thousand rupees at an online merchant this morning. Can you confirm whether this was you?

You

No, that wasn't me.

Caller

Thank you. I have blocked the card as a precaution. You will not be liable for that transaction.

You

How do I know this call is genuine?

Caller

That is a very fair question. We will never ask for your OTP, PIN or password, and I will not ask you for any code. If you prefer, please hang up and call us back on the number on your card. You can also visit your nearest branch. Your reference number for this call is 8829104.

You

Alright, that's reassuring.

Caller

Take your time, and you can discuss with your family before doing anything. A replacement card will reach you in five working days.
